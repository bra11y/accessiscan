C — Deep paper / parchment

Background #f4f1eb (warm parchment). Borders #c8c2b5 (antique). Accent #3d6b1a (deep olive-green). Warmest option — closest to aged newspaper/journal. Very distinct personality.

All three pass WCAG contrast minimums. The lime green (#c8f135) from dark mode is too low-contrast on any light bg — the accent must darken in light mode.
