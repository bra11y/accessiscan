A — URL input embedded in hero

The scan input is the hero CTA. User enters URL, scan runs, results expand below on the same landing page. Sign-up prompt appears inline after results.

  • Highest conversion — immediate action from landing
  • No navigation needed
  • Landing page becomes the product experience
  • More complex to build (page state management)

B — Dedicated /scan page (no auth)

Landing page CTA navigates to /scan which becomes publicly accessible. Full scan UI, full results. Sign-up prompt appears as a banner/modal after results load.

  • Cleaner separation of concerns
  • Easier to build — just remove auth middleware from /scan
  • Full-screen focus on the scan task
  • One extra click from landing page

C — Both: quick preview in hero → full results at /scan

Hero has a URL input that runs a quick scan and shows a summary score inline. A "Full Report →" button takes users to the dedicated /scan page with the complete results already loaded.

  • Best of both: immediate hook + full experience
  • Score preview in hero drives curiosity
  • More complex — two scan surfaces to maintain
  • Risk of double-scanning the same URL

All options: results are fully visible before any sign-up prompt. The gate only appears after results load.
