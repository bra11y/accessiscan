B — Banner below nav

A slim banner appears at the top of the results area when the scan completes. Left-border accent, inline CTA. Scrolls away as the user reads results.

  • Appears at natural reading start point
  • Scrolls out of view — low ongoing pressure
  • Clean, inline with results flow

C — Inline CTA woven into results

First 2 issues shown in full. Then an inline card: "+12 more issues — sign up to unlock." Remaining issues blurred below. Creates urgency without a modal.

  • Highest conversion pressure
  • Results teasing drives action
  • Slightly misleading if user expects "full results"
  • Could feel manipulative — use carefully

You said "full results before any gate" — A and B honour that fully. C partially gates. All can coexist (e.g., A sticky bar + B banner).
